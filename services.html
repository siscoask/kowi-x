Our Services

Seven Subsidiaries. One Mission.

Explore Our Seven Subsidiaries

Kowi X Cybersecurity & Consulting

Objective: To safeguard the digital landscape by delivering end-to-end cybersecurity solutions and strategic consulting services.

We provide ethical hacking, risk assessment, security architecture design, and digital policy advisory.

Our services help businesses and individuals strengthen security posture, achieve regulatory compliance (NDPR, GDPR), and build long-term digital resilience against evolving threats.

From penetration testing to incident response and security training, we protect what matters most.

Kowi X Academy

Objective: To build competent, future-ready tech professionals through practical and industry-aligned education.

We offer hands-on training in cybersecurity, networking, AI/ML, and software development.

Our curriculum includes real-world projects, mentorship from industry experts, and globally recognized certifications.

Graduates are equipped to solve complex problems, lead innovation, and thrive in Africa’s growing digital economy.

Kowi X Innovations

Objective: To serve as the creative and technical hub specializing in modern digital products.

We design and develop responsive websites, mobile apps, and custom software solutions.

Specialized in fintech platforms, social apps (including dating), and user-centric experiences.

Every product combines functionality, security, scalability, and aesthetic excellence.

Kowi X Foundation

Objective: To inspire social and community development through digital empowerment.

We run mentorship programs, digital literacy workshops, and youth tech bootcamps.

Focused on responsible technology use, personal growth, and positive social impact in underserved communities.

Partnering with schools, NGOs, and local leaders to bridge the digital divide.

Kowi X Media

Objective: To share technology insights and innovation stories through engaging content.

We produce videos, podcasts, blogs, and social media campaigns.

Covering cybersecurity awareness, tech trends, startup journeys, and digital transformation.

Building an informed, inspired community shaping Africa’s tech narrative.

Kowi X Health

Objective: To promote accessible and trusted healthcare through innovation and quality.

We supply verified medical drugs, wellness products, and health consultation services.

Integrated with digital platforms for telemedicine, prescription management, and home delivery.

Ensuring affordable, reliable, and efficient access to essential healthcare.

Kowi X Labs

Objective: To pioneer research and development in emerging technologies.

Exploring AI, quantum security, IoT, and blockchain applications.

Developing prototypes, filing patents, and creating future-ready solutions for African challenges.

Collaborating with universities and global tech leaders to push innovation boundaries.
